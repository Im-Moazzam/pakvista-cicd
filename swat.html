About Swat Valley

Located in the Malakand Division of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Swat Valley is celebrated for its breathtaking high-altitude alpine meadows, clear-water rushing rivers, and snow-capped peaks. Known globally as the "Switzerland of Pakistan", it offers an incredible mix of natural wonders, deep valleys, and historical archaeological sites stretching back to the ancient Gandhara civilization.

Must-Visit Attractions in Swat

Malam Jabba

The premier ski resort destination in Pakistan, famous for its winter sports festival, chairlifts, and panoramic snowy ridge lines.

Kalam Valley

A mesmerizing alpine enchanted forest zone located along the upper reaches of the Swat River, serving as the gateway to pristine glacial lakes.

Mahodand Lake

Accessible by four-wheel drive vehicles, this stunning, sprawling body of water is surrounded by massive fields of alpine wildflowers and dynamic waterfalls.
