City of Gardens

Lahore, where history, devotion, and culture meet.

Lahore is one of Pakistan's most celebrated cities, known for its grand Mughal landmarks, lively streets, and timeless artistic spirit. This page highlights three defining places that capture the city's character.

Why Lahore Stands Out

Lahore blends old and new in a way few cities can. You can walk from monumental Mughal architecture to bustling food streets, then end the day in a calm garden or courtyard. Its heritage is not only preserved in buildings, but also in the everyday rhythm of the city.

The three landmarks below represent Lahore's most memorable side: the grandeur of the Badshahi Mosque, the layered history of Lahore Fort, and the elegant symmetry of Shalimar Gardens.

Featured Attractions

Three landmarks that define the city's heritage and visual identity.

Religious Landmark

Badshahi Mosque

The Badshahi Mosque is one of the most iconic monuments in South Asia. Built during the Mughal era, it is admired for its vast courtyard, bold red sandstone, and elegant marble accents.

Its scale and symmetry make it a powerful symbol of Lahore's spiritual and architectural heritage.

Historic Fortress

Lahore Fort

Lahore Fort tells the story of centuries of royal life, defense, and artistic refinement. Its halls, gateways, and courtyards reflect the different rulers who shaped the city over time.

Visitors come here to experience layered history, detailed craftsmanship, and sweeping views of the old city.

Garden Heritage

Shalimar Gardens

Shalimar Gardens showcases the Mughal love for balance, water, and landscaping. Its terraced design, fountains, and carefully arranged walkways create a calm atmosphere that still feels graceful today.

The gardens remain one of the finest examples of classical Mughal garden planning in Pakistan.
