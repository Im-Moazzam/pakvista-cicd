Welcome to Hunza Valley

Surrounded by snow-capped peaks, crystal-clear rivers, and ancient forts, Hunza Valley is one of Pakistan's most breathtaking tourist destinations.

About Hunza

Located in Gilgit-Baltistan, Hunza Valley is famous for its majestic landscapes, friendly culture, and historical landmarks. Every year thousands of visitors travel here to witness the beauty of the Karakoram Mountains.

The valley offers stunning views throughout the year, especially during spring blossom season and autumn when the mountains are covered with vibrant colors.

Quick Facts

  • Region: Gilgit-Baltistan
  • Altitude: ~2,500 meters
  • Best Time: April–October
  • Known For: Mountains & Forts
